The Minimum Commitment Threshold AWS commitments are one of the largest savings levers in an AWS estate, but few teams have a repeatable way to set a Minimum Commitment Threshold , the safe floor for what you can actually reserve.. The mechanism is simple: commit to a level of usage for one or three years through Savings Plans or Reserved Instances , and AWS gives you a discount in exchange.
